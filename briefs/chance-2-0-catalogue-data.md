## Grade positioning (catalogue copy, for page intros)
- **Classic** — the iconic engraving in 236 shades; stock service. Supple, long-lasting, low-maintenance; all upholstery applications.
- **Mediflex** — healthcare; advanced antimicrobial + antiviral + biocompatible; withstands alcohol & bleach disinfectants; bi-elastic for complex shapes.
- **Bioblend** — 65% biobased (chestnut residues + organic cotton); circular design; durable, fire-resistant (Crib 5), contemporary colours.
- **Crib 5** — hospitality where safety is essential; Crib 5; durable, easy-clean, comfortable.
- **Crib 7 | B1** — stadiums, auditoriums, high-traffic public venues; the most stringent fire standards (Crib 7 / B1 / M1); exceptional durability.
- **Outdoor** — outdoor upholstery; extreme conditions; light fastness, high fire resistance, mould/stain/wear resistant.

## Build implications & open items
1. **Grades have their own colour cards** — NOT all subsets of one 236 palette. Classic (236) and Outdoor overlap heavily; **Mediflex** and **Crib 5 / Crib 7|B1** have many distinct colour names (e.g. Mediflex Anderson/Arpad/Aurora/Carnelian…; Crib 5 Almond/Bosque/Fortress…) not in the Classic range. → the colour metaobject must be the **union** of all grades' colours (~250–300 unique), each grade linking its own subset.
2. **Swatch/hex source is a gap.** This catalogue contains the **Classic** swatches (as images only, no hex) and every grade's **specs**, but NOT the swatch/hex for the performance-grade colours. To render real swatches we need either (a) hex values / swatch images per colour (all grades), or (b) build with names first and add swatches later. **Decision needed.**
3. **Specs are now datasheet-backed** — apply the tables above to each grade collection page (replace qualitative copy), and reconcile the Performance Fabrics + Medical pages (e.g. Mediflex antiviral 75.52% ISO 18184; Crib 5 = BS 5852 Crib 5 + DIN 4102 B2; Crib 7|B1 = Crib 7 + DIN B1 + NF M1).
4. **236 vs 235** — one Classic swatch had no extractable text label; reconcile against the physical colour card.
5. Confirmed from earlier: Outdoor "Pure" was a duplicate (→39 unique); Mediflex renames Desert→Dune, Ocean→Lagoon, Shale→Oasis.
