## 1. What changed — in a nutshell

CHANCE is no longer a single collection. It becomes an **umbrella brand of six sub-collections**, all sharing the same iconic CHANCE engraving (texture) but each tuned to a different performance grade. Each sub-collection is built on an existing Monteiro product:

| CHANCE 2.0 sub-collection | Built on (current product) | Positioning in one line |
|---|---|---|
| **CHANCE CLASSIC** | Chance | The 236-shade iconic texture; broad contract use. **Stock service.** |
| **CHANCE MEDIFLEX** | Mediflex | Healthcare; bi-elastic; antimicrobial + antiviral; disinfectant-proof. |
| **CHANCE OUTDOOR** | Ocean | Outdoor / marine; light-, weather- and mould-resistant. |
| **CHANCE BIOBLEND** | Peel | Sustainable; 65% biobased (chestnut by-products). |
| **CHANCE CRIB 7 \| B1** | Prosoft | High-traffic public venues; stadiums & auditoriums; top fire specs. |
| **CHANCE CRIB 5** | Portus | Hospitality where safety is essential; Crib 5. |

**Positioning line (cover):** *"CHANCE 2.0 — an iconic engraving offered in an unrivalled spectrum of colours that allows freedom of expression and individuality."*

The idea: **one look, six performance grades.** Same engraving across the family; pick the variant by the fire / durability / sustainability / hygiene requirement of the project.

## 2. The six sub-collections (detail)

### CHANCE CLASSIC  *(= Chance)*
- **Tagline:** *236 colours, one iconic texture. Supple, long-lasting, and low-maintenance.*
- **Description:** In an updated palette of **236 shades**, the iconic texture offers an unrivalled spectrum of colour. Countless options for all upholstery applications where durability, comfort and high performance are assured.
- **Stock service available** (the only variant offered from stock).

### CHANCE MEDIFLEX  *(= Mediflex)*
- **Tagline:** *Durable, bi-elastic coated fabric designed for intensive disinfection.*
- **Description:** High-performance coated fabric for **healthcare** environments, combining advanced antimicrobial protection with exceptional durability. Resists intensive cleaning protocols and alcohol- and bleach-based disinfectants while keeping a soft touch and refined appearance. Bi-elastic and versatile — adapts to complex shapes.
- **Construction:** easy-to-clean finishing · multilayer PVC coating\* · polyamide.  \**Antifungal, antibacterial and antiviral.*

### CHANCE OUTDOOR  *(= Ocean)*
- **Tagline:** *High-performance for extreme environmental conditions and intensive use.*
- **Description:** High-performance coated fabric for **outdoor** upholstery, built to withstand extreme conditions and intensive use. Excellent light fastness, high fire resistance and outstanding durability. Resistant to mould, stains and wear; easy-clean surface.

### CHANCE BIOBLEND  *(= Peel)*
- **Tagline:** *Biobased upholstery incorporating natural by-products.*
- **Description:** A sustainable upholstery solution made with **65% biobased content**, incorporating natural by-products from **chestnut processing**. Combines circular design with advanced coated-fabric technology — turning agricultural waste into a high-performance material. Durable, fire-resistant and available in contemporary colours.
- **Headline split:** 65% plant-based materials (incl. chestnut residues) · 35% PVC.

### CHANCE CRIB 7 | B1  *(= Prosoft)*
- **Tagline:** *Exceptional durability for high-traffic public venues.*
- **Description:** Developed for **stadiums, auditoriums and high-traffic public spaces.** Combines exceptional durability with seating comfort and complies with the most stringent fire-resistance standards. Resistant to abrasion, stains and wear; easy-clean surface for efficient maintenance.

### CHANCE CRIB 5  *(= Portus)*
- **Description:** A coated fabric for **hospitality** environments where safety is essential, complying with **Crib 5** fire-resistance standards. Durability with lasting comfort, excellent resistance to wear and intensive use, easy to maintain and pleasant to the touch.

## 5. Sustainability & certifications

- **Long-lasting — 10-year warranty.** Promoting a long life cycle reduces frequent replacement and waste.
- **100% vegan — VVV+,** the highest level of the Animal Free Fashion ethical rating by LAV; no animal-derived materials.
- **REACH compliant** — full compliance with the EU REACH regulation.
- **ISO 9001 & ISO 14001** certified organisation (quality + environment).
- **CHANCE BIOBLEND:** 65% biobased content from chestnut by-products; biobased content measured per ASTM D6866-22 Method B.

## 6. Notes & implications

- **Stock service:** only **CHANCE CLASSIC** is offered from stock; the other five appear to be made-to-order.
- **CRIB 7 | B1** is the outlier on two specs: narrower **rolls (25 m)** and a higher minimum **temperature (-10 ºC)** vs -40 ºC for the rest.
- **Each sub-collection = an existing product** (Classic↔Chance, Mediflex↔Mediflex, Outdoor↔Ocean, Bioblend↔Peel, Crib 7\|B1↔Prosoft, Crib 5↔Portus). This has knock-on implications for the website taxonomy, the Shopify catalogue, and the OKF knowledge bundle — those products may need to be re-grouped under a CHANCE umbrella.
