# Overview

Monteiro Fabrics is a Porto-based manufacturer of coated technical upholstery fabrics
(faux-leather / PVC-coated textiles) for the contract and residential interior market.
Fabrics are engineered for high-traffic environments — hospitality, healthcare, marine,
office, retail and public spaces — and are sold by the roll, with free samples available.

# Ranges

| Range | Positioning |
|-------|-------------|
| Essentials | Everyday durable coated fabrics, broad colour palettes |
| Pure Contract | Heavyweight, high-abrasion fabrics for demanding contract use |
| Natrue | Plant-based, more sustainable coated fabrics |
