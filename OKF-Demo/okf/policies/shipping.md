# Free samples

Monteiro Fabrics offers **free fabric samples**. To order:

1. Open a product or collection page and choose the colour(s) you want.
2. Click **Add to cart** — you can add more than one colour.
3. Open the cart, proceed to checkout, and enter your contact and delivery address.

Samples are dispatched within a few days. A physical catalogue can also be requested
from the colour options on a product page.
