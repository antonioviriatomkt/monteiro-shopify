# Free samples

Free samples **cannot be returned or exchanged**, as they are provided at no charge.
If a sample arrives **damaged or defective**, contact customer service to request a
replacement.

# Purchased items

Monteiro Fabrics' standard return policy applies to purchased items. For returns,
exchanges or refunds on purchased goods, contact the customer service team.
