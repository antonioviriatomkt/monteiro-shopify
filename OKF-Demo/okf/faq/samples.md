# Frequently asked questions

**How do I get a sample?**
Add the colours you want to the cart from any product page and check out — samples are free.
See [shipping & free samples](/policies/shipping.md).

**Are the fabrics suitable for outdoor or marine use?**
[Lima Collection](/products/lima-collection.md) is built for marine exterior and outdoor use.
Many other ranges are rated for marine interior.

**Do you have a sustainable option?**
Yes — the [Peel Collection](/products/peel-collection.md) uses 60% plant-based materials.

**Which fabrics are most hard-wearing?**
The Pure Contract range ([Mara](/products/mara-collection.md),
[Prosoft](/products/prosoft-collection.md), [Furna](/products/furna-collection.md)) and
[Chance](/products/chance-collection-1.md) reach ≥400.000 Martindale cycles.
