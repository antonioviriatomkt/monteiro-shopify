# OKF demo kit — Monteiro Fabrics

Two browser demos plus the real OKF bundle, all generated from Monteiro's live Shopify catalogue and store policies. Use these to pitch OKF as a paid "AI discoverability" service.

## What's here

- **`1-what-the-AI-sees.html`** — side-by-side: the messy raw HTML an AI scrapes today vs. the clean OKF file we publish instead (uses the real Beta Collection).
- **`2-ask-monteiro-chat.html`** — a working "Ask Monteiro" assistant that answers *only* from the OKF bundle and cites the source file for every answer. Runs offline in any browser.
- **`okf/`** — the actual spec-conformant bundle: 12 fabric collections, 3 policies, company + contact, FAQ, plus `index.md` and `log.md`.

## How to run the pitch (5 minutes)

1. **Open demo 1.** "This is what ChatGPT or Google's AI has to read about your Beta fabric today" (point at the left) "…and this is what we give it instead" (right). Land the line: *you did SEO for Google; this is the same move for the AI assistants that are becoming the new front door.*
2. **Open demo 2.** Let them type their own question — a fabric, a spec, "can I return a sample?". Every answer is correct and shows the exact OKF file it came from. "This is reading your catalogue, with no guessing — and it's also a preview of an assistant you could put on your own site."
3. **Close on the offer:** one-time bundle build + monthly retainer to keep it current (the catalogue changes, so the bundle must too), with an optional on-site assistant as the upsell.

## Honest framing

The bundle isn't yet hosted, so live answer-engines (ChatGPT, Perplexity) won't have crawled it during the meeting — that's why these are controlled demos that prove the *mechanism*. The live-hosted proof (publish at `okf.monteirofabrics.com`, then track AI-referral traffic over a few weeks) is the follow-up that converts them onto the retainer.

## Note on the data

Specs, policies and contact details are pulled from the live store. Prices were €0 across the catalogue (sample-led model), so price isn't surfaced. A couple of fire-certification lists were abbreviated for the demo files — the full per-collection certs would be carried over in a production build.
