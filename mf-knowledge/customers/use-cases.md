## Jeans — hospitality & QSR seating

* [Jeans Collection](/products/collections/jeans.md) is built mainly for **high-traffic hospitality
  and quick-service-restaurant (QSR) seating**, where its weight (930 g/m²) and ≥400.000 Martindale
  abrasion resistance stand up to intensive daily use.
* **McDonald's** uses Jeans in some of its restaurants — a strong durability proof point for QSR
  fit-outs.

  (sales conversations, internal decks) until confirmed. Jeans is also not yet listed on the website
