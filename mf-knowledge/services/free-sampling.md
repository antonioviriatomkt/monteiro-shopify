# Overview

Monteiro Fabrics offers **free fabric samples**. Customers choose colours on any product or
collection page, add them to the cart (multiple allowed), and check out with a delivery address.
Samples ship within a few days. A physical catalogue can also be requested.

# Why it matters

Sampling is the primary top-of-funnel motion: the catalogue is priced at €0 to drive swatch
requests and downstream contract enquiries.
