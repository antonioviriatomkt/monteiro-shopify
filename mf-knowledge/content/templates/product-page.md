# When to use

Writing or refreshing a collection's product page.

# Structure

1. **Name + one-line hook** (texture + key benefit).
2. **Overview** — what it is, range, ideal settings.
3. **Spec table** — composition, weight, abrasion, width, fire, antibacterial, colours.
4. **Applications** — segments it suits.
5. **CTA** — order a free sample.

# Voice

Follow [brand voice & tone](/brand/voice-tone.md). Lead with performance + ease of care; keep
claims backed by [certifications](/operations/certifications.md).
