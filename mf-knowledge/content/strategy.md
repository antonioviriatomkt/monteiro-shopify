> Drafted July 2026 from the brand layer + live site. **Confirm goals/targets with Ivana.**

# Goal
Build Monteiro's reputation with B2B specifiers and designers, and drive **free-sample requests**
(the site's core conversion) and qualified project enquiries — while reinforcing the brand as a
design-led, certified, worry-free coated-fabrics maker.

# Pillars
Content ladders up to the four brand [messaging pillars](/brand/messaging-pillars.md):
**Design · Performance · Sustainability · Service & trust.** Aim for a rough balance across the month
rather than leaning on one.

Reusable content types:
- **Collection / COTM** — colour stories, texture, new launches.
- **Project & case study** — Savoy Palace, Vila Foz, McDonald's, ArchDaily, stadium seating.
- **Sector spotlight** — healthcare, marine, mobility, hospitality, outdoor, office, public spaces.
- **Proof / education** — certifications, Martindale, fire, care & cleaning, "coated vs leather".
- **Sustainability** — Peel, Origin, Natrue/PU, biobased honesty, Animal Free.
- **Company / brand** — heritage (1917 / 1967 / 4th gen), partnerships, team, values.

# Cadence
- **Social: 2 posts/week**, each produced as **one Notion row** with both an Instagram (`Copy Meta`)
  and a LinkedIn (`Copy LinkedIn`) version. Instagram = design-led register; LinkedIn = technical
  register (same topic, adapted).
- **Blog:** website articles as planned (own Notion row, `Topic = Blog`), feeding LinkedIn and the
  newsletter.
- **Newsletter:** periodic, recapping news/projects/trends (see [email](/channels/email.md), stub).

# Where posts are produced
Everything is drafted and tracked in the **Notion "MF Content Planner"**, written directly by the
content workflow. Schema + procedure live in the WORK vault:
`Clients/Monteiro-Fabrics/reference/content-planner-notion.md` and
`Clients/Monteiro-Fabrics/playbooks/write-content-post.md`.

# Language
English by default (B2B). PT-PT/other on request or for locally-targeted pieces.
