# In one line

Monteiro sounds like a **confident Portuguese maker** who knows fabric inside out: warm and
design-led on the surface, technical and certified underneath. We earn trust with proof, not
adjectives — and we never oversell.

# The two registers

Monteiro speaks in one voice with two registers. Pick by channel/audience, not by mood.

| | **Design-led (default brand)** | **Technical (specifier)** |
|---|---|---|
| Where | Instagram, Pinterest, brand posts, collection launches, editorial | LinkedIn, healthcare/marine/mobility/contract topics, spec-driven posts, Performance Fabrics |
| Audience | Designers, architects, brand followers | Specifiers, procurement, technical buyers, contract manufacturers |
| Feels like | Warm, curious, tactile, a little playful | Credible, precise, understated, evidence-first |
| Site examples | *"Looks like textile. Performs like a pro."* · *"What if the Amazon rainforest could speak through fabric?"* · *"Fabric made from chestnuts."* · *"Easy peasy!"* | *"ISO 9001 & ISO 14001 Certified Organization."* · *"the longest guarantee on the market"* · *"a durable, easy-clean solution designed for high-traffic spaces"* |

Most social posts blend the two: a design-led hook, a technical proof point, a warm CTA.

# Principles

1. **Proof over hype.** Lead with a certification, a real project, or a number we can stand behind.
   Let the evidence do the persuading.
2. **Tactile and concrete.** Name the texture, the colour, the space it lives in. Show fabric in
   real settings over stock language.
3. **Plain and specific.** Short sentences. One idea per post. Prefer the clear word to the clever one
   (a light touch of wit is welcome, not required).
4. **Human and warm.** We're a fourth-generation family maker, not a faceless mill — "worry-free",
   "everyday partner", "let's work together".
5. **Quietly proud, never boastful.** We've dressed the Savoy Palace and supply McDonald's worldwide —
   we mention it as a fact, not a brag.

# Word choices

- **Always:** *coated fabrics*, *coated technical upholstery fabrics*, *coated upholstery fabrics*.
- **Never (in brand voice):** *faux leather*, *synthetic leather*, *leatherette*, *pleather*, or
  *vinyl* as the noun for the product. **SEO exception:** these terms may appear naturally in
  blog/web copy targeting search (see the site's own *"Leather or Coated Fabrics? That's the Wrong
  Question"*).
- **Signature phrases (approved, from site):** *"a lifetime company"* · *"specialists in coated
  fabrics since 1967"* · *"Made in Portugal"* · *"the longest guarantee on the market"* ·
  *"Worry-free"* · *"Tailormade"* · *DNA: Design · Innovation · Sustainability · Service.*
- **Spelling:** UK/European English (colour, organisation, specialise). B2B default language = English.

# Do / Don't

- ✅ "Portus BLT was chosen for the Vila Foz sun-lounger pillows — comfort that survives sun, salt and
  daily use." ❌ "The most amazing luxury fabric you'll ever see!!!"
- ✅ "400,000 Martindale. Fire-certified. Wipe-clean." ❌ Inventing a figure to sound impressive.
- ✅ One clear CTA: *Order a free sample.* ❌ Three CTAs and five links in the caption body.

# Guardrails

- **No invented or unverified figures/claims.** Only certified/verified numbers (see
  [certifications](/operations/certifications.md); quick list in the WORK vault's `brand-facts`).
  If unsure, leave it out.
- **No blanket "PFAS-free" / chemical claims** unless a sourced note supports them.
- Sustainability claims stay specific and honest — echo the site's own rigour
  (*"Biobased… but how much, really?"*), never greenwash.
