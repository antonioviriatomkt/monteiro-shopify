# Positioning statement (working)

**For** designers, architects and specifiers of demanding interior and mobility projects,
**Monteiro Fabrics** is the **Portuguese manufacturer of coated technical upholstery fabrics** that
combines **design-led collections** with **certified, high-performance materials** and a
**worry-free, lifetime service** — because we've made coated fabrics in our own European facilities
since 1967, now into the fourth generation.

**Vision (from the company):** *"To be the European reference in the production of coated technical
fabrics, with a strong focus on quality, service, functional innovation, and environmental
responsibility."*

# What makes it credible (reasons to believe)
- Made in Portugal, own facilities, European-only supplier base.
- ISO 9001 & ISO 14001; Animal Free LAV VVV+; 10-year warranty (longest on market).
- 29 collections across 9 ranges — everyday to specialist (medical, marine, mobility, sustainable).
- Landmark proof: Savoy Palace, Vila Foz, McDonald's worldwide, ArchDaily-featured projects.

# Frame of reference / differentiation
- **Category:** coated technical upholstery fabrics (PVC/PU) — *not* positioned as "faux leather".
- **vs. leather:** performance, cleanability, consistency, ethics and price without the drawbacks
  (the site reframes the whole debate: *"Leather or Coated Fabrics? That's the Wrong Question"*).
- **vs. large coated-fabric rivals** (Spradling, skai, Ultrafabrics): design breadth + colour, a
  genuine family-maker service story, and Made-in-Europe certification — competing on trust and
  design, not just volume. (Competitor detail: [competitors index](/competitors/index.md), still stubs.)

# Audience (who we're for)
Primary B2B specifiers and buyers: interior designers & architects, contract furniture makers,
procurement, and sector OEMs (healthcare, hospitality, marine, mobility, office, public spaces).
