# Boilerplate descriptions (pick by length)

**One line**
Monteiro Fabrics — Portuguese manufacturer of coated technical upholstery fabrics, made in Portugal since 1967.

**Short (≈40 words, site-sourced)**
Monteiro Fabrics grew from a passion for quality coated fabrics and was built on a legacy that dates
back to 1917. Now in the fourth generation as a family business, we develop fabrics with exceptional
design, function, sustainability and performance.

**Medium (≈70 words)**
Monteiro Fabrics is a Porto-based manufacturer of coated technical upholstery fabrics (PVC- and
PU-coated) for contract, residential and mobility interiors. Made in Portugal in our own facilities
from European-only suppliers, our fabrics are ISO 9001 and ISO 14001 certified and backed by the
longest warranty on the market. Across 29 collections and 9 ranges, we pair design-led colour with
certified performance — and free samples, worry-free.
