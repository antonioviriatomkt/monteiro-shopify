# The four pillars (mapped to the DNA)

## 1. Design — "Fabric with a point of view"
Coated fabrics that look and feel like considered textile, in Europe's broadest, most colourful
palettes. Design-led collections, some by name designers (Amazónia by Nini Andrade Silva), chosen
for landmark interiors.
- **Proof:** Chance — *"Europe's most colourful coated fabric collection"* · Straw — *"Looks like
  textile. Performs like a pro."* · Savoy Palace & Vila Foz (Nini Andrade Silva) · Canteen /
  ArchDaily · colour matching, lab dips, pilot samples.
- **Say it to:** designers, architects, editorial, Instagram/Pinterest.

## 2. Performance — "Built for demanding spaces"
Technical upholstery engineered for high-traffic, high-hygiene, high-exposure environments —
abrasion, fire, cleanability, sun and salt.
- **Proof:** Martindale up to 400,000 · wide fire certifications · ISO 9001 · easy-clean / wipe-clean ·
  sector-specific lines (Electra medical, Mediflex, Ocean marine, Pure Move mobility).
- **Say it to:** specifiers, procurement, healthcare/marine/mobility/contract, LinkedIn.
- ⚠️ Cite only verified figures; ranges vary by collection — check the collection page.

## 3. Sustainability — "Honest, certified, made in Europe"
Environmental responsibility backed by certification and real biobased chemistry, communicated
without greenwash.
- **Proof:** ISO 14001 · Animal Free LAV VVV+ · Natrue/PU plant-based lines · Peel (chestnut-based) ·
  Origin (from olives) · the honest *"Biobased… but how much, really?"* stance · European-only
  supplier base.
- **Say it to:** all audiences; especially sustainability-driven briefs and procurement.

## 4. Service & trust — "A worry-free, lifetime partner"
A fourth-generation family maker that de-risks the project: longest warranty on the market, tailormade
capacity, seamless ordering, free samples.
- **Proof:** 10-year warranty (*"the longest guarantee on the market"*) · Tailormade · *"Worry-free"* ·
  Made in Portugal, own facilities · free samples, stock service · since 1917, coated fabrics since
  1967 · McDonald's official supplier worldwide.
- **Say it to:** procurement, returning buyers, brand/company posts.

# Claims discipline
- Use figures **only** where verified; if a number isn't in a sourced note, don't state it.
- No "PFAS-free"/chemical claims without a source. Sustainability = specific and provable.
- "Coated fabrics", never faux/synthetic leather (SEO exception: blog/web).
