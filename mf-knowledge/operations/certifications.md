# Overview

Monteiro fabrics are tested to a range of fire, abrasion and hygiene standards; the Natrue range
is positioned around sustainable/bio-based content. Exact certifications vary by collection.

# Categories

* **Fire reaction** — EN 1021-1/-2, BS 5852 (Crib 5), NFP92-503 (M2), IMO 2010 FTC, FMVSS302, UNI 9175.
* **Abrasion** — ISO 5470-2 (Martindale), up to ≥400.000 cycles.
* **Hygiene** — antibacterial / antifungal; antimicrobial (Prosoft).
* **Sustainability** — plant-based content (Natrue / Peel).

# Company certifications & guarantees

* **ISO 9001:2015** — quality management system. Certified organisation; management system certified
  by **TÜV Rheinland**.
* **ISO 14001:2015** — environmental management system (also TÜV Rheinland).
* **Animal Free — LAV VVV+** — Monteiro uses no animal-derived materials and holds the **highest**
  level (VVV+) of the Animal Free Fashion ethical rating issued by **LAV**.
* **EU REACH** — products manufactured in compliance with Regulation (EC) No 1907/2006.
* **10-year warranty** — on stock-service materials and current products carrying the 10-year
  warranty logo (the longest guarantee on the market).
* **Made in Portugal** — produced in Monteiro's own facilities; European-only supplier base.

Full certificates are published in the company [Press Kit](https://www.monteirofabrics.com/pages/press).
