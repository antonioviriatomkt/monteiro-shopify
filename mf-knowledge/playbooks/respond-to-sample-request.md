# Steps

1. Confirm the colours/collections requested and the delivery address.
2. Check stock; flag any unavailable shades and suggest nearest alternatives.
3. Dispatch within a few days; include a catalogue if relevant.
4. Log the contact for follow-up — a sample request is a contract-sales lead.
5. Follow up after delivery to offer specification help.
