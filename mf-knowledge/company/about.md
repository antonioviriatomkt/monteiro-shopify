# Overview

Monteiro Fabrics is a Porto-based manufacturer of **coated technical upholstery fabrics**
(PVC- and PU-coated textiles) for contract, residential and mobility interiors.
All fabrics are **made in Portugal** in the company's own facilities, sourcing exclusively from
European suppliers. Monteiro is **ISO 9001** (quality) and **ISO 14001** (environmental)
certified and backs stock-service materials with a **10-year warranty**. Fabrics are sold by the
roll, with free samples.

# Identity

> *Our unique experience and know-how, along with the combination of traditional manufacturing with
> updated technology, enables us to be present in demanding markets around the world. Going on our
> fourth generation as a family business, we develop fabrics with outstanding design, function and
> performance.*

Monteiro Fabrics is a **"lifetime company"** built on a legacy that dates back to **1917** and is
now run by the **fourth generation** of the family. It is part of the family-owned **Monteiro Ribas
group** and has specialised in coated fabrics **since 1967**. See [History](/company/history.md).
