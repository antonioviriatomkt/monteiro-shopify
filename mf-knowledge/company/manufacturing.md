# Overview

Monteiro produces **coated upholstery fabrics**: a PVC (or, for the Natrue / PU ranges, partly
plant-based or polyurethane) surface layer over a polyester or cotton backing textile, finished to
leather-like and textile-like textures across ranges. Every fabric is **made in Portugal** in the
group's own **state-of-the-art facilities**, combining **traditional manufacturing know-how with
updated technology**. Production and dispatch are based in Porto.

# Made in Portugal, European supply

* **All fabrics manufactured in Portugal**, in Monteiro's own facilities — not outsourced.
* **Rigorous supplier selection**: the company partners **exclusively with European providers** to
  secure consistent, top-tier quality and shorter, more controllable supply chains.
* As part of the fully integrated **Monteiro Ribas group**, Monteiro controls the process
  end-to-end, which underpins its sustainability claims (e.g. local raw materials for the
  [Origin](/products/collections/origin.md) and [Peel](/products/collections/peel.md) collections).

# Capabilities

* **Colour matching** — state-of-the-art colour-matching technology can match the colour of a
  customer's sample; the company can also provide a **lab dip** for approval or a **pilot sample**
  before production.
* **Tailormade solutions** — bespoke capacity answers specific design and technical requirements,
  delivering functional and aesthetic solutions beyond the stock range. See
  [Tailormade](https://www.monteirofabrics.com/pages/tailormade).
* **"Worry-free" ordering** — customers describe Monteiro as a worry-free partner: a deliberately
  seamless ordering process with prompt delivery and minimal friction, supported by the
  [Stock Service](https://www.monteirofabrics.com/pages/stock-service-1).

# Quality & environment

Manufacturing operates under **ISO 9001** (quality) and **ISO 14001** (environmental) management
systems — see [Certifications & guarantees](/operations/certifications.md). Stock-service materials
and current products carrying the 10-year warranty logo are backed by a **10-year guarantee**, the
longest on the market.
