# Purpose

> *The purpose of Monteiro Fabrics is to produce and sell coated fabrics with a focus on excellence, following the motto of doing more and doing it better.*

We strive to offer a product that has an impact on our customers' business, inspires them, contributes decisively to their success, and makes them see MF as an everyday partner and facilitator.

Four traits are ingrained in our DNA and set our work apart: **Design, Innovation, Sustainability, Service.** We prioritise creativity and innovative solutions in both products and services, keep a proactive attitude tailored to each client and to the society we are part of, and remain committed to protecting the environment and the resources the world provides.

# Mission

> *To produce coated fabrics of excellence that are innovative, safe, and sustainable, with a strong focus on the customer, our people, and respect for the environment.*

# Vision

> *To be the European reference in the production of coated technical fabrics, with a strong focus on quality, service, functional innovation, and environmental responsibility.*

# Values

| Value | What it means |
|-------|---------------|
| **Focus on the customer** | We place the customer at the heart of all our decisions, striving to understand their needs and anticipate expectations. The relationship of trust we build is the foundation of our sustainable growth. |
| **Caring for our people** | We believe our success begins with our people. We foster an environment in which every employee feels valued, respected, and supported in their personal and professional development. |
| **We are a team** | We work collaboratively, sharing responsibilities and successes. It is through the union of skills, ideas, and perspectives that we find the best solutions. |
| **Making things happen** | We take a proactive, results-oriented approach. We make commitments, face challenges, and deliver — always with rigor and a strong sense of responsibility. |
| **Safety above all & respect for the environment** | Ensuring the safety of our employees and the communities in which we operate is a non-negotiable priority. We act with environmental responsibility, adopting practices that protect the planet and promote a more sustainable future. |
