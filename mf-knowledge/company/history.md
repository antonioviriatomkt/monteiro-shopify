# Origin

Monteiro Fabrics grew from a passion for quality coated fabrics, built on a legacy that dates back
to **1917**. It is part of the **Monteiro Ribas group**, a family-owned industrial group founded
that year in Porto, Portugal, which began life as a **tannery**. Today Monteiro Fabrics is run by
the **fourth generation** of the family and is a specialist manufacturer of coated technical
upholstery fabrics — *"a lifetime company."*

The combination of **traditional manufacturing know-how with updated technology** is the through-line
of the company's history and the reason it can serve demanding markets around the world.

# Timeline

| Year | Milestone |
|------|-----------|
| **1917** | The Monteiro Ribas group — a family-owned group — is founded in Porto. It starts as a **tannery**. |
| **1950s** | The tannery becomes a **market leader**. |
| **1960s** | The group begins **diversifying and expanding its range**; production of **vinyl (PVC) coated fabrics** begins. |
| **1967** | Monteiro's specialism in **coated fabrics** is established — the date the brand carries forward (*"specialists in coated fabrics since 1967"*). |
| **1970s** | The first **PU (polyurethane) coated fabric** is launched, alongside other products. |
| **Today** | Now in its **fourth generation** as a family business, Monteiro Fabrics develops fabrics with exceptional design, function, sustainability and performance, made in Portugal and sold worldwide. |

# Today

Monteiro Fabrics manufactures all of its fabrics in **Portugal**, in the group's own
state-of-the-art facilities, partnering exclusively with European suppliers. It is **ISO 9001** and
**ISO 14001** certified, holds the **Animal Free LAV VVV+** ethical rating, and backs its
stock-service materials with a **10-year warranty**. The company operates a strong global network,
with its head office in Porto and a commercial office in Germany — see [Contact](/company/contact.md).
