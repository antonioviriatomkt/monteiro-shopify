# Overview

The June 2026 redesign repositions the Monteiro Fabrics website from a product/shop-led
storefront into a **solution-led B2B** experience — organised around what a buyer is trying to
solve (by sector / application) rather than around the fabric catalogue alone. Every change is
staged on an unpublished sandbox theme so the live site is never touched until sign-off.

# Goal

- Reposition from "browse fabrics" to "find the right fabric for your sector / project".
- Lift Google search visibility and AI-answer (AI Overviews / ChatGPT / Perplexity) citation for
  sector and collection pages.
- Evolve the existing look rather than depart drastically from it.

# Design constraints (recorded)

- **Evolve, don't replace** — reuse existing sections and the theme's `.button` / `.button.alt`
  styling; "it can't look too distant from the current website".
- **Copy colour:** use black **#111111**, not brand red #E32C2B. Brand blue is **#2F519B**.
- Keep e-commerce / free-sampling present but **secondary**.
- Don't repeat homepage sections on interior pages.
- **No invented technical figures** — performance claims stay qualitative unless backed by a datasheet.
