# Overview

Monteiro Fabrics for the **mobility industry** — fabrics engineered for transport interiors that
prioritise safety, comfort and style, exceeding OEM benchmarks for seat abrasion and wear
resistance. The dedicated ranges are [Pure Move](/products/ranges/pure-move.md) and
[Pure Convertible](/products/ranges/pure-convertible.md).

# Sectors in this group

* [Automotive](/market/segments/automotive.md) - Automotive interiors — OEM-grade seat abrasion and wear resistance, meeting automotive fire standards (FMVSS302, UN ECE R118).
* [Motorcycle](/market/segments/motorcycle.md) - Motorcycle seating — grip, UV and weather resistance with transport fire compliance.
* [Aviation](/market/segments/aviation.md) - Aircraft cabin interiors — FAR 25.853 flammability compliance.
* [Mass Transportation](/market/segments/mass-transportation.md) - Bus, coach, train and metro seating — high abrasion resistance and transport fire standards.
* [Recreational Vehicles](/market/segments/recreational-vehicles.md) - Campervans, motorhomes and caravans — durable, cleanable, comfortable upholstery.

* [Train](/market/segments/train.md) - rail/metro seating (proposed, pending confirmation).
