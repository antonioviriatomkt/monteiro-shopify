# Overview

Medical is the **high-performance tier of [Healthcare](/market/segments/healthcare.md)** — fabrics
engineered for operating rooms, intensive-care units and other highly sensitive or sterile clinical
environments, where antiviral / biocompatible performance and the most demanding cleanability are
required. The broader Healthcare sector covers general clinical and care settings; Medical is the
stricter, higher-performance subset.

# Monteiro collections for this sector

* [Mediflex Collection](/products/collections/mediflex.md) - antiviral, biocompatible (ISO 18184 / ISO 10993).
* [Electra Collection](/products/collections/electra.md) - electrostatic protection for ICUs, imaging rooms and operating theatres.
* [Flora Collection](/products/collections/flora.md) - PU leather alternative used in medical settings.
