# Market

The landscape Monteiro plays in. The website's new **"Our Sectors"** menu (MF_JUNE) is organised around
the sectors below, in menu order. "Collection" here means a Shopify sector collection — see
[Terminology](/references/terminology.md).

## Our Sectors (new menu)

* [Hospitality](/market/segments/hospitality.md) - Hotels, bars and lounges — durable, cleanable, fire-rated upholstery with broad colour palettes.
* [Automotive](/market/segments/automotive.md) - Automotive interiors — OEM-grade seat abrasion and wear resistance, meeting automotive fire standards (FMVSS302, UN ECE R118).
* [Office](/market/segments/office-contract.md) - Workplace and contract seating — hard-wearing fabrics in timeless shades.
* [Marine Interior Fabrics](/market/segments/marine.md) - Boat and yacht interiors — marine-grade durability with IMO fire compliance.
* [Healthcare](/market/segments/healthcare.md) - Clinics, hospitals and care homes — antimicrobial, easy-to-disinfect, alcohol-resistant upholstery; several collections add antiviral and biocompatible properties.
* [Medical](/market/segments/medical.md) - High-performance healthcare upholstery for operating rooms and highly sensitive clinical areas.
* [Recreational Vehicles](/market/segments/recreational-vehicles.md) - Campervans, motorhomes and caravans — durable, cleanable, comfortable upholstery.
* [Outdoor](/market/segments/outdoor.md) - Exterior and semi-exterior settings — terraces, poolside and garden furniture needing UV, weather and water performance.
* [Public Spaces](/market/segments/public-spaces.md) - High-traffic public and contract interiors where durability and fire compliance matter most.
* [Fashion](/market/segments/fashion.md) - Fashion, accessories and leather-goods applications using coated fabrics.
* [Aviation](/market/segments/aviation.md) - Aircraft cabin interiors — FAR 25.853 flammability compliance.
* [Mass Transportation](/market/segments/mass-transportation.md) - Bus, coach, train and metro seating — high abrasion resistance and transport fire standards.
* [Train](/market/segments/train.md) - Rail and metro seating fabrics (proposed sector — to be confirmed with Ivana). **(pending)**
* [Motorcycle](/market/segments/motorcycle.md) - Motorcycle seating — grip, UV and weather resistance with transport fire compliance.
* [Fitness](/market/segments/fitness.md) - Gyms and wellness spaces — sweat-, alcohol- and abrasion-resistant antibacterial upholstery.
* [Residential](/market/segments/residential.md) - Home interiors — leather-look and textile-look fabrics that are family- and pet-friendly.
