# Overview
LinkedIn is Monteiro's **B2B credibility channel** — where the technical/specifier register leads
(see [voice & tone](/brand/voice-tone.md)). Purpose: build trust with specifiers, procurement and
partners through proof, projects and expertise. Part of the **2 posts/week** cadence (shared with
[Instagram](/channels/instagram.md)); the same topic is usually adapted for both, written more
technically here.

# Audience
Interior designers & architects, contract furniture manufacturers, procurement, sector OEMs
(healthcare, marine, mobility, hospitality, office, public spaces), industry partners. English.

# Cadence & formats
- **~2 posts/week** (aligned to the Notion planner and [content calendar](/content/calendar.md)).
- **Formats:** single image, carousel/document, occasional video; project case studies and
  certification/standards explainers perform well. Link out to blog articles and collection pages.
- **Angles:** proof points (certifications, Martindale, fire), project case studies (Savoy Palace,
  Vila Foz, McDonald's, ArchDaily), sector deep-dives, sustainability with rigour
  (*"Biobased… but how much, really?"*), "coated vs leather" reframes, company/partnership news
  (e.g. Kaizen Institute), Performance Fabrics.

# Post structure (body = `Copy LinkedIn`)
Lead with a technical proof point or a real buyer problem → 2–4 short paragraphs (point, evidence,
relevance to contract/technical buyers) → one clear CTA. ~120–160 words. Hashtags optional and
sparing (0–3 relevant tags; see [hashtag library](/content/hashtag-library.md)).

- **CTAs:** *Talk to our technical team* · *Request a sample* · *Read the article* · *See the project*.

# Do / Don't
- ✅ Evidence-first, understated, specific. ✅ Explain a standard or a spec clearly.
- ❌ Hype, emoji-spam, hashtag walls. ❌ "Faux leather" (SEO exception is blog only). ❌ Unverified numbers.
