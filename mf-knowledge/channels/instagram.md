# Overview
Instagram is Monteiro's **design-led shop window** — texture, colour and real installed projects.
Primary register is the warm/design-led voice (see [voice & tone](/brand/voice-tone.md)), with one
credible proof point per post. Part of the **2 posts/week** social cadence (shared with
[LinkedIn](/channels/linkedin.md)); most posts run on both.

# Audience
Interior designers, architects, specifiers, contract furniture makers, and design-interested
followers. International; English-first captions (B2B default).

# Cadence & formats
- **~2 posts/week** (aligned to the [content calendar](/content/calendar.md) and the Notion planner).
- **Formats** (map to the planner's `Content Type`): Single Image, Carousel, Reel, Story, Photo.
  Favour real fabric texture and installed settings over stock renders.
- **Recurring series ideas:** Collection of the Month (COTM), colour stories, project spotlights
  (Savoy Palace, Vila Foz, Canteen), sector features (healthcare, marine, outdoor), sustainability
  (Peel/Origin), "coated vs leather" myth-busting.

# Post structure (caption = `Copy Meta`)
Hook → benefit → one light spec/proof → single CTA. ~60–125 words. Keep hashtags in the planner's
`Hashtags` field, not stuffed in the caption body. See the [Instagram template](/content/templates/instagram-post.md).

- **CTAs:** *Order a free sample* · *Link in bio* · *DM us* · *Explore the collection*.
- **Hashtags:** build from the [hashtag library](/content/hashtag-library.md) (~8–15, core + sector + attribute).

# Do / Don't
- ✅ Show the material and where it lives; name the colour/collection. ✅ One clear CTA.
- ❌ "Faux leather" (brand voice). ❌ Invented specs. ❌ Five CTAs and a wall of hashtags in-caption.
