# Overview

A circular fabric sample is rubbed against a standard abradant in an expanding figure-eight
pattern under set pressure. Cycles are counted until the endpoint (e.g. thread breaks or surface
wear). Coated contract fabrics commonly target 150.000–400.000+ cycles.
