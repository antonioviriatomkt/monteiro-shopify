# Glossary

| Code | Meaning | Note |
|------|---------|------|
| PVC | Polyvinyl chloride | A synthetic thermoplastic material made by polymerizing vinyl chloride. Is one of the most widely used plastics in the world.Its durability, versatility, and recyclability make it an excellent material for coated fabrics across many industries, from furniture and automotive to healthcare and hospitality. |
| PES | Polyester | Backing textile for strength |
| CO | Cotton | Backing textile, softer hand |
| Plant-Based | Bio-based content | Used in the Natrue range (e.g. Peel) |
