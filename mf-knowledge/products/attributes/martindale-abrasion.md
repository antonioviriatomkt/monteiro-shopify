# Overview

Abrasion resistance is measured by the **Martindale** test (ISO 5470-2): a fabric sample is
rubbed in a figure-eight motion until wear appears; the result is given in cycles. Higher = more
hard-wearing.

# How Monteiro's ranges compare

| Indicative threshold | Suitability | Monteiro ranges |
|------|-------------|------------------|
| ≥150.000 cycles | General contract & residential | Most Essentials collections |
| ≥400.000 cycles | Heavy-duty / severe contract | Pure Contract, Chance, Peel |
