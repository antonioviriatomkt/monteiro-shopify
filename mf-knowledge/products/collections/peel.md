# Overview

Peel is Monteiro Fabrics' flagship **sustainable** collection (the **Natrue** range): a coated
upholstery fabric **made from chestnut-harvest waste**, built on **60% plant-based materials** and
reaching **65% bio-based carbon content** (ASTM D6866-22 Method B), that still delivers full
contract durability (400.000 Martindale). Its theme is *"65% Pure Nature."* Nature-inspired,
sustainable style — sold by the roll, available from stock, and offered as a
[free sample swatch](/services/free-sampling.md).

# The story — fabric from chestnuts

Portugal is a world reference in chestnut harvest. Chestnut processing generates by-products that
would otherwise be discarded — wood, flowers, leaves, shells, barks and burs. Working with local
partners, Monteiro collects these wasted scraps, crushes and mills them into a fine powder, and
transforms them into a fashionable upholstery material. As a crop by-product, **no additional water
or land** is required to source the raw material.

# How it's made (8 stages)

1. Chestnuts are harvested
2. The fruit is peeled
3. The waste is collected
4. Leftovers are milled into a fine powder
5. Chestnut residues are mixed with paste
6. The manufacturing process follows
7. A finished upholstery fabric is obtained
8. Ready to ship worldwide

# Sustainability

* **60% plant-based materials** — chestnut waste, **organic cotton**, and natural oils — combined
  with 40% PVC.
* **65% bio-based carbon content**, measured per **ASTM D6866-22 Method B** (the share of carbon
  from natural vs synthetic / petrochemical sources).
* A technologically viable product ready for large-scale production **without compromising
  performance** — all technical requirements for upholstery end-use are met.
* **100% vegan / Animal Free** (LAV VVV+); **phthalate-free**; **EU REACH** compliant. Made in
  Portugal.

# Performance

Peel covers all technical requirements for upholstery end-use, reaching **400.000 Martindale rubs** —
matching the high durability expected of Monteiro's conventional contract fabrics — while remaining
flame-retardant, abrasion-resistant and long-lasting.
