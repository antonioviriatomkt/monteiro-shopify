# Overview

Amazónia is a **designer collection co-created by internationally acclaimed interior designer
[Nini Andrade Silva](https://www.niniandradesilva.com/en/) exclusively for Monteiro Fabrics**,
inspired by the Amazon rainforest. Its tagline is *"Inspired by Nature, Crafted for Tomorrow."*
Part of the **Specialty** range, it is a coated technical upholstery fabric sold by the roll,
available as a [free sample swatch](/services/free-sampling.md) and with a downloadable catalogue.

# The story

Nini Andrade Silva journeyed into the heart of the Amazon rainforest to create the narrative
behind the collection. The palette is a tribute to the life the forest shelters — the iridescent
feathers of exotic birds, the vivid petals of tropical flowers, the lush green of dense vegetation,
and the rich, grounding hues of the forest floor.

The collection is positioned as more than a showcase of design: it is framed as a creative homage
to the Amazon and a statement of **environmental responsibility and ecological awareness**. It was
born from Nini's desire to bring the bright, eccentric colours of her travels into interiors, with
Monteiro Fabrics — four generations of craftsmanship combined with modern manufacturing, plus
expertise in colour and sustainability — as the manufacturing partner.

# Designer — Nini Andrade Silva

Born in Funchal, Madeira, Nini Andrade Silva is one of the most prestigious names in international
interior design and has elevated Portuguese design onto the global stage.

> "I draw inspiration from the landscape, the wind, and the land, delving into history and engaging
> with locals to uncover the true essence and roots of each place." — Nini Andrade Silva

Her recent recognitions include the **Savoy Palace Hotel** in Madeira, which won the *World Travel
Award for Europe's Leading Luxury Hotel* (2024) — a project that featured Monteiro Fabrics' coated
fabrics.
