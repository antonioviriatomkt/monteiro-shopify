# Overview

Patio Collection is part of Monteiro Fabrics' **Outdoor** range — Indoor style, outdoor performance. A coated technical
upholstery fabric supplied by the roll, available as a [free sample swatch](/services/free-sampling.md).

A leather-grain coated fabric built for outdoor use — hydrostatic-tested (ISO 811), UV- and weather-stable, rated for −30 ºC cold-crack — with indoor-grade softness and styling.
