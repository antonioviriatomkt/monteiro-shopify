# Overview

Marina Collection is part of Monteiro Fabrics' **Marine** range — Outdoor performance, indoor style, textile look. A coated technical
upholstery fabric supplied by the roll, available as a [free sample swatch](/services/free-sampling.md).

Credentials: 100% vegan · [Animal Free LAV VVV+](/operations/certifications.md) · REACH compliant · phthalate free · anti-microbial & anti-fungal · inherently flame retardant · light resistant · abrasion resistant · long-lasting. Easy to clean with mild detergent and a damp cloth.

A woven-look marine textile — lighter and thinner than the Patio and Siesta grades — with salt- and chlorinated-water colour-fastness, perspiration/saliva/blood/urine fastness, and an M2 (NF P 92-507) fire rating for healthcare and transit interiors.

# Colours

17 shades: Pure White, Wheat, Linen, Hay, Corn, Peach, Amber, Pecan, Ocean, Sky, Jeans, Indigo, Mist, Steel, Lime, Graphite, Black.
