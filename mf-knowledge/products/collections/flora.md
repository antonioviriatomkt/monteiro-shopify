# Overview

Flora Collection is part of Monteiro Fabrics' **PU** range — Leather alternative. Soft hand, subtle sheen. A coated technical
upholstery fabric supplied by the roll, available as a [free sample swatch](/services/free-sampling.md).

Credentials: 100% vegan · [Animal Free LAV VVV+](/operations/certifications.md) · REACH compliant · phthalate free · suitable for toys & childcare articles (Directive 2005/84/EC) · inherently flame retardant · 10-year warranty. Easy to clean with mild detergent and a damp cloth.

A PU (polyurethane) leather alternative — soft hand and subtle sheen on a cotton backing, with a 10-year tropical-climate hydrolysis guarantee against the cracking and peeling that affects lesser PU.
