# Overview

Siesta Collection is part of Monteiro Fabrics' **Outdoor** range — Indoor style, outdoor performance. A coated technical
upholstery fabric supplied by the roll, available as a [free sample swatch](/services/free-sampling.md).

Credentials: 100% vegan · [Animal Free LAV VVV+](/operations/certifications.md) · REACH compliant · anti-microbial & anti-fungal · inherently flame retardant · light resistant · abrasion resistant · long-lasting. Easy to clean with mild detergent and a damp cloth.

A woven-look marine textile — salt- and chlorinated-water colour-fast, UV-stable and abrasion-resistant — styled for boat and outdoor seating.
