# Overview

Ocean Collection is part of Monteiro Fabrics' **Pure Contract** range — Exciting colours, elegant texture, outdoor soul. A coated technical
upholstery fabric supplied by the roll, available as a [free sample swatch](/services/free-sampling.md).

# Specifications

From the Ocean summary data sheet (full Data Sheet on request — no DC number printed).

| Attribute | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Composition | PVC 80% · PES 20% (polyester) |
| Weight | 690 g/m² ± 5% |
| Thickness | 1,00–1,20 mm |
| [Abrasion resistance](/products/attributes/martindale-abrasion.md) | 400.000 Martindale (ISO 5470-2) |
| Colour fastness | Rubbing, alcohol, bleach, oil, sun cream, water, chlorinated & sea water, perspiration, saliva, blood & urine |
| Light fastness | Artificial light (ISO 105-B02) |
| Roll width | 1,37–1,40 m |
| Antibacterial / antifungal | Yes (Sanitized® — JIS Z 2801, ISO 846 A) |
| [Fire resistance](/products/attributes/fire-certifications.md) | FMVSS302 · EN 1021-1+2 · NFPA 260 (clause 6.1) · Cal. TB 117 (Section 1) · IMO 2010 FTP Code Part 8 · NF P 92-507 (M2) |

Credentials: 100% vegan · [Animal Free LAV VVV+](/operations/certifications.md) · REACH compliant · phthalate free · suitable for toys & childcare articles (Directive 2005/84/EC) · UV / outdoor light resistant. Easy to clean with mild detergent and a damp cloth.

The polyester base, sea- and chlorinated-water fastness and sun-cream resistance make Ocean the outdoor / marine grade of the range. Colour range and full technical data are on the complete Data Sheet (free swatches on request).
