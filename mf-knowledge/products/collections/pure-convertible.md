# Overview

Pure Convertible is part of Monteiro Fabrics' **Pure Convertible** range — Exclusive for automotive and retail applications. A coated technical
upholstery fabric supplied by the roll, available as a [free sample swatch](/services/free-sampling.md).

# Specifications

Datasheet **ED.01 V2** (Mobility). Pure Convertible is Monteiro's convertible-top / soft-top automotive range. The datasheet groups its articles into three technical profiles:

| Article group | Weight | Thickness | Composition | Abrasion ([Martindale](/products/attributes/martindale-abrasion.md)) |
|---------------|--------|-----------|-------------|----------------------|
| Jean | 580 g/m² ± 5% | 0,70–0,90 mm | PVC 84% · PES 10% · CO 6% | 150.000 |
| Lona · Trek | 380 g/m² ± 5% | 0,50–0,70 mm | PVC 76% · PES 16% · CO 8% | 150.000 |
| Cover · Hilux · Sport · Strada · Toscana | 820 g/m² ± 5% | 0,95–1,15 mm | PVC 76% · CO 24% | 150.000 |

All profiles share: roll width 1,37–1,40 m · rolls ± 35 m · low-temperature (cold-crack) resistance · ethyl alcohol & bleach resistance.

[Fire resistance](/products/attributes/fire-certifications.md): FMVSS302 · EN 1021-1+2 · Reg. 118 (Annexes 6, 7 & 8).

Credentials: 100% vegan · [Animal Free LAV VVV+](/operations/certifications.md) · REACH compliant · low-temperature (cold-crack) resistant · 10-year warranty. Easy to clean with mild detergent and a damp cloth.
