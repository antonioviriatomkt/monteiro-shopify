# Overview

Electra is Monteiro Fabrics' groundbreaking **permanently antistatic** healthcare upholstery fabric,
engineered for environments where electrostatic buildup is a hazard — **intensive-care units,
imaging rooms and operating theatres**. It safely channels static electricity away to prevent
electric shocks and protect sensitive medical equipment, combining ESD safety with hygiene, comfort
and — unusually for antistatic fabrics — refined colour. Part of the **Specialty** range and a core
fabric for the [Medical](/market/segments/medical.md) sector; sold by the roll and available as a
[free sample swatch](/services/free-sampling.md).

# Why it exists

Medical facilities such as ICUs, imaging rooms and operating theatres must be designed to prevent
electrostatic buildup: uncontrolled static discharge can cause mild electric shocks to patients and
staff, interfere with sensitive medical equipment, and pose risks to people and devices. Electra was
engineered specifically for this challenge, using state-of-the-art chemical functionalization for
conductivity.

# Antistatic performance

* **Permanently antistatic / ESD-protective** — conductive properties are stable and **independent
  of humidity**. Electrical resistivity **10⁵–10⁶ Ω**.
* Surface treated to inhibit microbial growth and resist staining from bodily fluids.
* Easier to clean and disinfect; resistance to dust and particle accumulation keeps surfaces
  cleaner for longer, reducing maintenance.

# Bi-elastic comfort & weldable precision

* **Bi-directional elasticity** follows the curves of the body and the contours of the furniture —
  improving comfort, making complex upholstery shapes easier, and reducing tension on seams and
  stress points.
* **Weldable** — the thermoplastic coating allows adjacent materials to be joined by heat welding:
  simpler assembly, more durable seams and a clean, clinical finish with fewer points of failure.

# Biocompatibility

Non-irritating and non-cytotoxic per **ISO 10993-5** and **ISO 10993-10** — safe interaction with
living tissues for environments where patient well-being is paramount.

# Design & colours

Electra breaks free from black-only antistatic norms with a sophisticated palette of deep, muted
tones that elevate clinical environments.

**9 shades:** Black, Grey, Dark Grey, Pewter, Umber, Deep, Navy, Fir, Mogno.
