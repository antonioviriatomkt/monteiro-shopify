# Overview

Origin is one of Monteiro Fabrics' **sustainable** collections (the **PU** range): a coated fabric
that transforms nature's waste — **olive pits and sugar-crop residues** — into a high-performance
material with stunning design and vibrant colour. It is produced from a **water-based formulation**
of bio-based waste combined with a **natural-fibre (cotton) base**, has a pleasant, smooth touch,
and is **72% bio-based**. Sold by the roll and available as a [free sample swatch](/services/free-sampling.md).

# The story — a circular economy from olive waste

Portugal is one of the world's largest producers of olive oil, so olive pits are an abundant
by-product. Based on a **circular-economy model**, rather than discarding millions of tonnes of
olive pits each year, Monteiro recycles this waste — together with sugar-crop residues — into soft,
durable upholstery fabric. Using **local raw materials** substantially reduces the carbon footprint,
and as a fully integrated industrial group (all production in its own facilities), Monteiro controls
the process end-to-end.

# Composition & process

A **water-based** coating made from bio-based waste, combined with a natural-fibre base. The full
make-up:

* **53% natural fibres**
* **19% plant-based materials** — from sugar-crop residues, oil-crop production residues, and olive-processing waste
* **28% polyurethane**

This gives **72% bio-based carbon**, measured per **ASTM D6866** (the share of carbon from natural
sources vs synthetic / petrochemical sources).
