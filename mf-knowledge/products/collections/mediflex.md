# Overview

Mediflex is Monteiro Fabrics' dedicated **healthcare / medical** coated upholstery fabric — a
**bi-elastic**, easy-clean fabric engineered for the most demanding clinical environments. It is
antifungal, antibacterial and antiviral, compatible with alcohol and bleach disinfection, and
biocompatible with living tissue. Part of the **Pure Contract** range and a core fabric for the
[Medical](/market/segments/medical.md) sector; sold by the roll, available from stock and as a
[free sample swatch](/services/free-sampling.md).

# Construction

A multilayer build engineered for hygiene and conformability:

* **Easy-clean finishing** on top.
* **Antimicrobial multilayer PVC coating** (antifungal, antibacterial, antiviral).
* **Polyamide** backing.

It is **bi-elastic** — stretching in two directions to follow organically flowing or curved soft
shapes — and is made **without harmful phthalates**.

# Hygiene & cleanability

* Compatible with **alcohol and bleach** cleaning solutions for highly effective disinfection.
* Resistant to **bodily fluids, lotions and creams**; colour-fast to water, chlorinated and sea
  water, perspiration, saliva, blood and urine.
* Integrated hygiene protects against bacteria, viruses, dust mites and mould, keeping surfaces
  odour-free. **Antiviral** (ISO 18184); antibacterial / antifungal.

# Biocompatibility

Non-irritating and non-cytotoxic, demonstrated per **ISO 10993-5** (in-vitro cytotoxicity) and
**ISO 10993-10** (irritation / delayed-type allergies) — safe interaction with living tissues for
environments where patient well-being is paramount.

# Durability, warranty & ethics

* ≥400.000 Martindale abrasion resistance; backed by a **10-year warranty**.
* **Animal Free** — certified by LAV with the highest **VVV+** ethical rating (no animal-derived
  materials). Made in Portugal by an **ISO 9001 & ISO 14001** certified organisation.
