# Overview

Jeans Collection is part of Monteiro Fabrics' **Pure Contract** range — a heavy, cotton-backed
coated fabric with a denim-inspired look, engineered mainly for **hospitality** and high-traffic
**restaurant / QSR** seating, where its weight and abrasion resistance stand up to intensive daily use.
A coated technical upholstery fabric supplied by the roll, available as a
[free sample swatch](/services/free-sampling.md) and held in the **stock service** (not yet listed on the website).
