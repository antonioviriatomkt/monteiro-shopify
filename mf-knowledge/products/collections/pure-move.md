# Overview

Pure Move Collection is part of Monteiro Fabrics' **Pure Move** range — Premium fabric crafted for mobility and transportation. A coated technical
upholstery fabric supplied by the roll, available as a [free sample swatch](/services/free-sampling.md).

# Specifications

Datasheet **ED.01 V1** (Mobility). Pure Move is Monteiro's mobility line — a family of polyester-backed and bi-elastic coated fabrics for transport interiors. The datasheet groups its articles into four technical profiles:

| Article group | Weight | Thickness | Composition | Abrasion ([Martindale](/products/attributes/martindale-abrasion.md)) | Bi-elastic |
|---------------|--------|-----------|-------------|----------------------|------------|
| Alvaro · Ascari · Evoque · Frank · Mansel · Norman · Renzo · Senna | 660 g/m² ± 5% | 0,90–1,10 mm | PVC 87% · PES 8% · CO 5% | 400.000 | — |
| Arata PES · Nascar PES · Philip PES | 640 g/m² ± 5% | 0,95–1,15 mm | PVC 87% · PES 13% | 400.000 | — |
| Frank PES | 680 g/m² ± 5% | 1,10–1,30 mm | PVC 87% · PES 13% | 150.000 | — |
| Alden BLT · Cross BLT · Norman BLT · Run BLT · Santiago BLT | 600 g/m² ± 5% | 1,10–1,30 mm | PVC 88% · PES 12% | 150.000 | Yes |

All profiles share: roll width 1,37–1,40 m · rolls ± 35 m · antibacterial / antifungal (More than clean) · low-temperature (cold-crack) resistance · ethyl alcohol & bleach resistance · colour fastness to water, chlorinated & sea water, perspiration, saliva, blood & urine, and to artificial weathering & light.

[Fire resistance](/products/attributes/fire-certifications.md): FMVSS302 · EN 1021-1+2 · Cal. TB 117 (Section 1) · NFPA 260 (clause 6.1, Class I) · IMO 2010 FTP Code Part 8 · Reg. 118 (Annexes 6, 7 & 8) · FAR 25.853 — the Alvaro group adds ÖNORM A 3800-1 & ÖNORM B 3825; the bi-elastic BLT group carries Reg. 118 (Annexes 6 & 7) without FAR or ÖNORM.

Credentials: 100% vegan · [Animal Free LAV VVV+](/operations/certifications.md) · REACH compliant · low-temperature (cold-crack) resistant · colour-fast to perspiration, water, chlorinated & sea water · UV / artificial-weathering & light resistant · 10-year warranty. Easy to clean with mild detergent and a damp cloth.
