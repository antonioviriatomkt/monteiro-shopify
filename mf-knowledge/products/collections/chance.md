# Overview

Chance is Monteiro Fabrics' iconic **Essentials** fabric — billed as *"Europe's Most Colourful
Coated Fabric Collection."* It is built around a single signature engraving — one iconic texture —
offered across an exceptionally broad palette, from soft muted neutrals to vibrant jewel tones and
bold statement colours, giving designers freedom of expression and individuality. A premium PVC
upholstery fabric, sold by the roll or by the metre, and available as a
[free sample swatch](/services/free-sampling.md).

> **Palette: 236 shades (current).** The public site and catalogue still state **228** — pending
> update. Chance is published across **7 store pages** to hold the full palette.

# Positioning — hospitality & contract

Chance is one of the most specified **hospitality fabrics in Europe**, chosen by architects and
interior designers for environments where aesthetics and durability are equally non-negotiable.
Engineered for intensive use — restaurant seating, hotel lobbies, bar counters and high-traffic
public areas — its ≥400.000 Martindale abrasion resistance keeps upholstered pieces looking good
through years of daily use. Specified from independent restaurants to international hotel chains.

# Easy to clean & long-lasting

* Cleans with nothing more than a damp cloth and mild soapy water — no specialist products. Resists
  scratches and fluids, preserving the finish far longer than textile alternatives.
* **Long-lasting** with a **10-year warranty** — fewer replacements, less waste.

# Hygiene, fastness & compliance

* Extensive colour-fastness: water, chlorinated & sea water, saliva, acid & alkaline perspiration,
  blood and urine (grey scale); light & artificial weathering (blue scale).
* Skin-irritation tested (ISO 10993-10); hydrostatic pressure (ISO 811); element migration (EN 71-3);
  antibacterial / antifungal.
* Manufactured free from harmful substances; fully **EU REACH** compliant ((EC) No 1907/2006).
* **100% vegan / Animal Free** — LAV **VVV+** (highest ethical rating). Made in Portugal.
