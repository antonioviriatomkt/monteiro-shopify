# Overview

Straw is a Monteiro Fabrics coated upholstery fabric with the **warmth and look of textile and the
strength of a coated fabric** — *"Looks like textile. Performs like a pro."* Its tagline is
*"Generation-Proof | Built to Last."* A durable, easy-clean solution positioned for **contract,
hospitality and other high-traffic interiors** where style, longevity and ethical credentials all
matter. Part of the **Pure Contract** range; sold by the roll and available as a
[free sample swatch](/services/free-sampling.md).

> **Specify with care:** abrasion is **50.000 Martindale** — lower than other Pure Contract fabrics.
> It is a textile-look decorative fabric; for the heaviest-traffic seating, pair with a higher-abrasion
> collection.

# The high-performance alternative to traditional textiles

Designers and specifiers increasingly want the appeal of textile with performance built for
high-use environments. Straw was developed for exactly that: a **textile-like surface texture** with
a softer, more natural look and feel, **without compromising resistance or cleanability** —
resisting abrasion, fire and fluids with reliability well beyond traditional textiles in demanding
shared spaces. The textile-like appearance is achieved by **texturing and shading the surface of a
multilayer PVC coating**, while preserving cleanability.

# Character & sustainability

* **Predominantly handmade process** — each article naturally shows greater **colour variation
  between batches**, an intended characteristic that enhances individuality; for matched projects,
  order from a single batch.
* **10-year warranty** — the extended lifespan reduces waste and replacement footprint.
* **100% vegan / Animal Free** (LAV VVV+); **phthalate-free**; **light-resistant**; **EU REACH**
  compliant. Made in Portugal.
