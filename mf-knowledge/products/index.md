# Products

Monteiro Fabrics coated upholstery fabrics. Each collection is one concept; sold by the roll and available as a free sample swatch.

> **Note:** here a "collection" = a **fabric line** (a Shopify Product, and what the website header calls "Collections"). For the *sector* sense of "collection", see [sectors](/market/index.md). Full disambiguation: [Terminology](/references/terminology.md).
