# Overview

Coated upholstery fabrics are valued for being easy to clean. General guidance below; always
confirm specifics per collection and test in an inconspicuous area first.

# Routine care

* Wipe regularly with a soft, damp cloth.
* For tougher marks, use mild soap and water; rinse and dry.
* Many collections carry alcohol resistance (grey scale) and antibacterial / antifungal
  properties, supporting disinfection in healthcare and hospitality settings.
